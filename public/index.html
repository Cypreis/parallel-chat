What a decision does
  • It is a rule for the model. Every new answer, reply and refresh in its scope is told to follow it, and it wins over anything the cards said before.
  • Scope: This chat applies only here; All chats applies everywhere.
  • Cards written before it are not rewritten by themselves. The ones you pick are marked Out of date; press Refresh to rewrite them with the decision.
  • Unpin it and the model stops following it; the out-of-date marks go away.

Want to remember something without the model acting on it? Use Notes.
